# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- **Pebble Time, Time Steel and Time Round** too, as well as the Time 2 and Round 2. They get a lighter 3D model (they give an app half the RAM) and close-ups made for their screens.
- **Bigger floor tiles**, the same size as the wall panels. Your test chamber carries over: each new tile takes the four small tiles it covers as its quarters.
- **The test chamber on the phone** is zoomed out to show whole every tile the watch can show.
- **24-hour dial** setting: the hour wedge goes round once a day, a wedge per hour, midnight at the top.

## 1.1.0

- **Knock it over:** flick your wrist again while the turret is zoomed out and it falls over firing, shuts down, and a claw carries it away and brings in a new one.
- **It can see you:** a new setting, the chance of the turret seeing you. It locks on and opens fire; knock it over before it gets you.
- **Defective turrets** from Portal 2, with their own voice lines: no bullets, and settings for the chance of one turning up, catching fire and exploding.
- **78 voice lines** on the Pebble Time 2 (39 for each turret), plus the firing, shutdown, fire and explosion sounds.
- The camera pans either way, and the turret rocks either way as its panels open.
- The close-up eye view shows a detailed picture of the turret that dissolves into the 3D model as it zooms out.
- Smoother sound (no more stutter while it shoots) and faster drawing.
- **Portal Turret Lite** (`lite/`): the same watchface without the defective turret, about half the size, so it installs faster.

**Installing:** Portal Turret is a big app (about 1.2 MB). The install may say it failed while the phone is still sending it: wait a minute longer, it usually installs anyway. If it really didn't, try Portal Turret Lite.
